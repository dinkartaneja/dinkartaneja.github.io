The system links it on its own

The score is 14.2. It is above the limit of 13 for auto link. Three records become one linked view. No steward task is made. No person spends time on it.

Why the limits matter
Before
Stewards check too many cases

When the limits are not set well, too many cases go to stewards. Some cases are clear matches. Some cases are clear non matches. Stewards still have to check all of them. That takes many hours.

The work
Set the limits with care

The engine is tested with real pairs of records. The limits sit where the proof is strong. High scores link on their own. Middle scores go to a person to check. Low scores do not link. Try the sliders above. That is the job.

After
The system does the clear cases

The system now does most of the work. It links clear matches on its own. It keeps clear non matches apart. Stewards see only the hard cases. That takes less time. It costs less. Links happen fast.
